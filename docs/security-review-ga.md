## Platform boundary changes since the prior review

Two landed changes move the boundary this ledger describes, so read every row
against them rather than against the July platform assumptions.

  [#1440](https://github.com/copse-dev/agent-pane/pull/1440) (`24d4ff97`) added

  [`project-sandbox/state.ts`](../src/main/project-sandbox/state.ts) is now

  be read as “macOS and Linux”; **Windows** is now the only unsandboxed
  platform, alongside a failed ASRT init on any platform.

  without a prompt.** [#1431](https://github.com/copse-dev/agent-pane/pull/1431)
  (`0a04b5e4`) is a real change to the approval boundary H1 and #581 were closed
  against, and is recorded as N1 below.

## GA disposition

No finding may move to **accepted** without a recorded owner, date, rationale,
and review date. That acceptance still does not waive a `ga-blocker` issue. A GA
waiver must separately name the issue/finding, approver, date, bounded release,
expiry/re-review trigger, and compensating controls.

For the reviewed revision:

- Human security review: **pending**.
- Release-owner sign-off: **pending**.
- Waivers: **none**.
- GA decision: **blocked** by the open entries above; this document does not
  close or waive them.

The two GA blockers named by the prior ledger are resolved: #662 and #664 are
closed, and both were verified against source on this revision rather than taken
from their issue state. The open `ga-blocker` issues that remain — #507 and #802
— are release-engineering items outside this ledger's scope; they are named here
so the GA decision is not read as security-only.

Before every release candidate, follow the security-review gate in
[`release-checklist.md`](./release-checklist.md) (reached from
[`releasing-macos.md`](./releasing-macos.md)): update the reviewed SHA/date,
recheck source plus issue/PR state, link new regressions, and record human
sign-off or explicit bounded waivers. Any security-relevant change after the
reviewed revision invalidates sign-off until the ledger is refreshed.

Two review habits earned their place this round and should carry forward:

- **Verify closures against source, not issue state.** #662 is closed and the
  filed defect really is fixed, but a later PR reverted the containment half; only
  reading `terminal-service.ts` surfaced N2.
- **Diff the security-relevant surface since the last reviewed SHA.** N1 landed
  as a productivity change, not as a security change, and would not have been
  found by re-checking the existing rows alone.
