Annotation tool strip

Today's strip is 504px wide but breaks onto a second row in any pane narrower than about 1,010px. It is centred with left: 50%, and an auto-width box placed that way may only use the space to its right, which is half its host. The swatches are 104px of that width.

Five strips over the same pane. Drag the width, then draw: right-click a tool in A or C, or click the one already selected, to change ink.

Theme

What would change

  • One row, always. width: max-content and flex-wrap: nowrap on the strip. The layer already watches its host with a ResizeObserver; the same callback picks the widest layout that fits: every tool with "Send to agent", then "Send", then the four shapes behind one button, and under about 280px the separators go too.
  • Ink on demand. The four swatches move into a popover on the drawing tools, opened by right-click or by clicking the tool that is already selected. Ink stays one colour shared by every tool, as today, and each mark still records its own.
  • The selected tool shows the ink. An underline in the current colour replaces the pressed swatch as the standing cue, and the tooltip names the gesture.

Open questions

  1. Is right-click plus click-again findable enough (A), or should one ink dot stay on the strip (B, 30px)?
  2. Should the shapes fold only when the pane is tight (A, B) or always (C)? Always is one layout to learn; folding costs a second click to change shape.
  3. The fix alone (second strip) is a one-line change and could ship ahead of the rest.
