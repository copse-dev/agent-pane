Note and Caution are both circles today, so at a glance they differ only by the mark inside. The solid columns give Caution an octagon — the stop-sign silhouette reads before the glyph does, and before the colour does for anyone who cannot separate red from blue.

The Copse icon set All 28 from src/renderer/dom/icons.ts — lucide-style outlines on a 24×24 grid, stroked in currentColor. The callout glyphs above have to sit beside these without looking like a different family.

The set is entirely outline at stroke 2 (.ui-icon), which is the tension in going solid for callouts: solid is right where a 16px glyph is the sole carrier of severity, but it makes the five alert marks the only filled shapes in the app. Two ways to keep one family — treat filled-vs-outline as meaningful (status marks are filled, chrome is outline), or drop back to outline for callouts and accept the weaker signal.
