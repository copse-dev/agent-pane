# Code of conduct

Copse adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. This page says how it applies here; the Covenant's text is the standard.

## The short version

Be welcoming, be patient, and assume good faith. Critique code, designs, and decisions, not the
people who made them. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sexualised
content, and publishing someone's private information without their permission are not acceptable,
in any form.

## Where it applies

Everywhere the project is represented: issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions in the
[copse-dev](https://github.com/copse-dev) repositories, the project's channels on other services,
and anywhere someone is acting on the project's behalf.

## Reporting

Email [security@copse.dev](mailto:security@copse.dev) with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Include
links or screenshots where you can. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and
answered as soon as practical. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so in the subject and it
will be handled with that conflict in mind.

Do not report a code-of-conduct problem in a public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a private correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact and
whether the behaviour continues. Comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break
this code may be edited, hidden, or removed.
