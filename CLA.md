## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the **CLA** check asks you to sign. Post
this sentence, exactly, as a comment on the pull request:

> I have read the Copse CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub account, the comment, and its timestamp are the signature record.
The Maintainer then adds you to `.github/cla-signatures.json`, and later pull
requests from you pass the check without a new comment.

Every commit in the pull request must be authored by a GitHub account that has
signed. If a commit's email address is not linked to your GitHub account, the
check cannot tell who wrote it; add the address to your account under
**Settings → Emails**, or rewrite the commit with an email that is linked.
